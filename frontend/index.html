About the Portal

The National Land Acquisition & Management System (NLAMS) is an end-to-end digital governance platform implemented by the Department of Land Resources (DoLR), Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, ensuring transparent implementation of RFCTLARR Act 2013 and PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan.
